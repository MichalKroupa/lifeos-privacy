Valqerio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Valqerio is a personal finance and vehicle management application. Your privacy is important, and Valqerio is designed to keep your personal data private.

Data Collection

The developer of Valqerio does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data from the app. Valqerio does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Data Stored in Valqerio

Information you enter into Valqerio, such as financial transactions, budgets, planned payments, vehicle information, service records, and application settings, is stored for the purpose of providing the app's functionality.

iCloud and CloudKit

Valqerio uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronize your data between your Apple devices. Valqerio uses the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple account. The developer does not operate a separate server or backend for storing your Valqerio data and does not have access to the contents of your private Valqerio records.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Apple Shortcuts and Wallet Automations

Valqerio can receive transaction information that you explicitly pass to the app through Apple Shortcuts or personal Wallet automations. This information is processed by Valqerio and stored as part of your private Valqerio data. It is not transmitted to the developer.

Notifications

Valqerio may schedule local notifications for features such as planned payment reminders. Notification scheduling is performed using Apple's system services.

Data Export and Deletion

Valqerio provides tools for exporting your Valqerio data and for deleting application data. Removing Valqerio data from the app may also propagate those deletions to your devices through iCloud.

Third-Party Services

Valqerio does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Apple system services such as iCloud, CloudKit, notifications, and Shortcuts may be used to provide app functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Valqerio functionality or data-handling practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided with the Valqerio App Store listing.
